Meet your coach

Hi, I'm Natalie

I was diagnosed with ADHD in 2022, and it took time to process. A year later I joined a group coaching session and everything changed. We recognised each other's stories, we were honest, and we really connected. I left with an exhilarating sense of belonging, and that's what inspired me to train as an ADHD coach.

Understanding my ADHD showed me how much I'd been carrying that never belonged to me: the self-blame, the shame, the confusion, and every reason I'd found for not moving forward. Once I knew my brain worked differently, I could finally offer myself compassion, and the courage to go after a life I'd only dared to dream about.

I love helping people tackle their barriers and limiting beliefs, and spot the best parts of themselves.

For 18 years I've worked with a national mental health charity, supporting people through exactly the things ADHD makes hard: starting and finishing tasks, impulse control, emotional dysregulation, shame spirals, self-trust, relationships, prioritising and organising. I'm also a qualified Social Worker, with 28 years' experience across addiction, offending behaviour, housing and mental health.

I hear what isn't said

I have a cochlear implant, and it's made me an exceptional listener. I notice what's said and, spookily, what isn't. In coaching, that's often where the real story is.

    Frequently asked questions

    About coaching

    What happens in a coaching session?

    Each session is guided by your agenda. We look at what's going on for you, get curious about your patterns and what's in the way, and find a next step that's genuinely doable for your brain. You leave with something practical to try, not a lecture.

    How much does coaching cost?

    £60 per 60-minute session, booked as a block of six (£360). Payment is upfront or per session. Further sessions can be booked afterwards if you'd like ongoing support. The first 30-minute chat is always free.

    Online or face to face?

    Either. We can meet by video wherever you are, or face to face in my garden studio just outside Stowmarket. Just say which you'd prefer when we have our free chat.

    Do I need a diagnosis?

    No. Whether you're diagnosed, on a waiting list or just wondering, you're welcome exactly as you are.

    Is this therapy?

    No, this is coaching, and I stay within coaching boundaries. Coaching is practical: where you are now, where you want to be and how to get there. Therapy helps you understand and heal from what has happened.

    About retreat days

    Do I need to be creative?

    Not at all. Creative exploration is optional and open to everyone. Some days are full of fun and chatter, others are a chance to unwind and find stillness. We'll see what unfolds.

    What's included, and what should I bring?

    Just yourself, plus any project you'd like to focus on. All materials, refreshments and a vegetarian or vegan lunch are provided. Let me know about dietary needs in advance. We can try painting, collage, batik or origami mobiles, and there's Suffolk countryside right outside if you'd like a walk.

    Is the studio accessible?

    The property is single-level with a few small steps, and there's a small step into the studio through a patio door. Inside it's quiet, calm and sensory-friendly, and I'm happy to adapt the space to your needs where I can.

    However we work together, I'll make sure you feel safe and listened to. People pleasing and masking can be left at the door.
